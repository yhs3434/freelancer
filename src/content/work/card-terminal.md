## The situation

Finlogistec, the company behind the six merchant apps in my [payments case study](/work/payments), also needed its payment app on a card terminal. I built the Android app for the Verifone X990.

## The terminal

The X990 is a handheld card terminal from Verifone that runs Android. It reads cards by chip, swipe or tap, and it prints receipts on a built-in printer.

## What I built

<div data-todo="card-terminal-built">

- Card payments through the terminal's own card reader.
- Cancelling a payment from the day's list.
- Printed receipts for the shop and the customer.
- The day's sales on the terminal screen.
