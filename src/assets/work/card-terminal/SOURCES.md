# Image sources

`placeholder.svg` is a neutral stand-in drawn for this site. It renders with `data-todo="cover"`, so `STRICT=1 npm test` fails until it is replaced.

Replace it with a photo or screenshot of the app running on the Verifone X990 that the owner took. Verifone's own product photos are copyrighted and are not used here. When the photo is in, point `cover` in `src/content/work/card-terminal.md` at it, write `coverAlt`, and delete `coverTodo: true`.
