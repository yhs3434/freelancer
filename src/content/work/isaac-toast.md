## The app

Isaac Toast is a Korean toast chain. Its app lets customers order ahead and pay, then collect stamps that turn into coupons. It went live on Google Play in December 2024.

Beaver Works builds kiosks, POS, table ordering and the chains' own ordering apps for Korean restaurant brands. I was the only developer on the Isaac Toast app, which is written in React Native for iOS and Android.

## What I built

<div data-todo="isaac-toast-built">

- Ordering and checkout, including card payments and Toss Pay.
- Stamps and the coupons they turn into.
- Over-the-air updates, so a fix can reach users without waiting for store review.

</div>

## Outcome

As of November 2026, the app has over 100,000 installs on Google Play and a 4.8 rating from about 1,100 ratings on the App Store. It is still live, and its latest update went out in November 2026.
