## The apps

Isaac Toast is a Korean toast chain. Its app lets customers order ahead and pay, then collect stamps that turn into coupons.

Beaver Works builds kiosks, POS, table ordering and the chains' own ordering apps for Korean restaurant brands. The ordering app is written in React Native, and I worked on it as one of the engineers on the app team.

## My part

<div data-todo="ordering-my-part">

- Ordering and checkout, including card payments and Toss Pay.
- Stamps and the coupons they turn into.
- Over-the-air updates, so a fix can reach users without waiting for store review.

## Paik's Coffee from the same code

In June 2026 Theborn Korea replaced the Paik's Coffee app with a new one built from the Isaac Toast code. The old app had been on Google Play since 2018, so the new one went out as an update to the same listing and reached everyone who already had it installed.

With the new app, Paik's Coffee members moved to Theborn Korea's shared membership, which puts several of its brands in one app.

## Outcome

As of November 2026, the Isaac Toast app has over 100,000 installs on Google Play and a 4.8 rating from about 1,100 ratings on the App Store. The Paik's Coffee listing has over a million Google Play installs, counted since 2018. Both apps still get updates. The latest went out in October 2026 for Paik's Coffee and in November 2026 for Isaac Toast.
